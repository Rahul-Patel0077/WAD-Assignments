About Sundar Pichai

Technology Executive & Innovator

Sundar Pichai is an Indian-American technology executive who serves as the Chief Executive Officer of Google and Alphabet. He was born on July 12, 1972, in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India. He completed his early education in India and later moved to the United States for higher studies. Pichai joined Google in 2004 and worked on several important products and services.

He became widely recognized for his leadership in the development and growth of Google Chrome and Chrome OS. His career at Google progressed through several senior leadership positions, eventually leading to his appointment as CEO of Google in 2015. In 2019, he also became CEO of Alphabet.

Under his leadership, Google has continued to focus strongly on artificial intelligence, cloud computing, search technology and digital products. Pichai is considered one of the most influential technology leaders in the world.
